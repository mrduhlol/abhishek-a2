## Users

## Product Purpose

Showcase Abhishek's projects and the enthusiasm he brings to building them. Success means visitors can explore his work and get a sense of his passion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The portfolio is an existing React and Vite website. The seven projects for the new terrain world are github-widgets, Stikr, Inkora, DocuType, Voxlink, Orbit, and Black Jack. Each project gets a distinct terrain and a short introduction on the main site; its main action opens a dedicated project subdomain, to be supplied by the user.
